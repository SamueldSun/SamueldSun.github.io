projects

autonomous inspection robot

designed and built a six-wheeled autonomous robot for industrial pipe inspection. the system uses lidar for navigation, multiple cameras for visual inspection, and a custom suspension system to handle rough terrain. the chassis is machined from aluminum with 3d printed components for complex geometries.

[image: completed robot on test track with visible suspension and sensor array]

key challenges included power management for extended operation, thermal management of electronics in an enclosed space, and designing a robust mechanical structure that could withstand industrial environments. the project required integration of mechanical design, embedded systems, and sensor fusion algorithms.

[image: CAD render showing internal component layout and wiring]

custom mechanical keyboard

designed a fully custom mechanical keyboard from the ground up, including the PCB layout, case design, and plate mounting system. the case is CNC machined from aluminum with a custom anodized finish. the project involved learning PCB design, switch selection, and understanding the mechanical properties of different mounting styles.

[image: finished keyboard with custom keycaps and aluminum case]

the design process included extensive prototyping to get the feel and sound profile right. i experimented with different plate materials (aluminum, brass, polycarbonate) and mounting styles (gasket mount, top mount) to achieve the desired typing experience.

precision motion control stage

developed a three-axis precision positioning stage with sub-micron accuracy for laboratory automation. the system uses linear ball screws, precision ground rails, and stepper motors with microstepping drivers. i designed the mechanical structure to minimize deflection and thermal expansion effects.

[image: motion stage assembly with visible linear rails and ball screws]

the project required careful consideration of material selection, bearing preload, and backlash compensation. i wrote custom control firmware to implement closed-loop positioning using optical encoders. the stage is currently being used in a research lab for automated sample handling.

[image: close-up detail of ball screw and linear rail assembly]

automated test fixture

built an automated test fixture for validating electronic assemblies in a production environment. the fixture includes pneumatic actuators for component manipulation, custom probe cards for electrical testing, and a vision system for optical inspection. the mechanical design prioritizes reliability and ease of maintenance.

[image: test fixture with visible pneumatic actuators and probe assembly]

this project involved working closely with manufacturing engineers to understand production requirements and design constraints. the fixture has been in continuous use for over a year with minimal downtime, validating the robustness of the mechanical design.
