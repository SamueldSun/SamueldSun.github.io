about me

i'm a mechanical engineer with a focus on hardware development and systems integration. my background combines traditional mechanical design principles with modern manufacturing techniques and embedded systems.

[image: workspace with tools, CAD monitor, and prototype parts]

education

i hold a bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering with coursework in dynamics, materials science, manufacturing processes, and control systems. my senior capstone project involved designing and building an autonomous robotic system for industrial inspection.

skills

my technical skills span mechanical design, CAD modeling (solidworks, fusion 360), rapid prototyping (3d printing, CNC machining), embedded systems programming, and PCB design. i'm comfortable working with both traditional manufacturing methods and modern digital fabrication tools.

[image: close-up of hands working on circuit board or mechanical assembly]

interests

outside of professional work, i enjoy building custom mechanical keyboards, working on robotics projects, and contributing to open-source hardware initiatives. i'm always learning new fabrication techniques and exploring the boundaries of what's possible with accessible manufacturing tools.

i believe in the power of hands-on engineering—there's something valuable about physically building and testing your designs that can't be fully captured in simulation alone.
